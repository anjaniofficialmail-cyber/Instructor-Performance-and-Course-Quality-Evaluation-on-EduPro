# Instructor Performance and Course Quality Evaluation on EduPro

A data-driven analysis of instructor effectiveness and course quality on the EduPro online learning platform, built to help EduPro identify high-performing instructors, evaluate course design consistency, and address training gaps.

## Project Overview

EduPro lacked a structured way to evaluate instructor quality and course design. This project analyzes instructor and course data to answer key questions:
- What is the overall distribution of instructor ratings?
- Do instructors with more experience receive higher ratings?
- Is there a relationship between instructor rating and course rating?
- Which expertise areas consistently deliver high-quality courses?
- Are highly rated instructors associated with higher enrollments?
